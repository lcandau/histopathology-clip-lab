\section{PLIP zero-shot comparison}
\label{sec:results-plip}

\subsection{Setup}
\begin{itemize}
    \item PLIP (Huang 2023): CLIP on $\sim$200k pathology Twitter pairs.
    \item Loaded \texttt{vinid/plip}, evaluated zero-shot on same test split + prompts.
    \item Isolates pathology-pretraining contribution.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Headline numbers vs baseline}
\begin{table}[ht]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Baseline (general)} & \textbf{PLIP (pathology)} & \textbf{$\Delta$} \\ \hline
    Accuracy          & 0.6356 & 0.6980 & +0.062 \\ \hline
    Balanced accuracy & 0.6356 & 0.6980 & +0.062 \\ \hline
    Macro F1          & 0.6273 & 0.7004 & +0.073 \\ \hline
    Weighted F1       & 0.6273 & 0.7004 & +0.073 \\ \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Pathology pretraining contribution to frozen CLIP transfer.}
    \label{tab:plip-vs-baseline-global}
\end{table}

\subsection{Per-class}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Big gains on benign tissues (lung F1 0.67$\to$0.87; colon F1 0.67$\to$0.80).
    \item Cancer subtypes essentially tied (lung\_aca F1 0.48$\to$0.48; colon\_aca F1 0.55$\to$0.56).
    \item Lung SCC recall jumps (0.84$\to$0.93), precision drops (0.70$\to$0.68) $\to$ overcalls SCC.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Retrieval contrast}
\begin{itemize}
    \item I$\to$T R@1 = 0.6980 (matches accuracy).
    \item T$\to$I P@K \emph{worse} than the baseline (P@1: 0.40 vs 0.80; P@50: 0.53 vs 0.84).
    \item Reading: the baseline was trained on LC25000 $\to$ embedding space tuned for in-distribution ranking. PLIP zero-shot $\to$ wins classification, loses fine ranking.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Interpretation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Pathology pretraining $\approx$ +7.3 macro-F1 on frozen CLIP transfer.
    \item Gain in benign-vs-malignant, not in subtype discrimination.
    \item Image clusters in the shared UMAP separate visibly better than the baseline (Figure~\ref{fig:plip-umap}), but the prompt markers still collapse --- domain pretraining helps the image side, not the (still-degenerate) text side.
    \item Feature adaptation confirmed as the bigger lever in \S\ref{sec:results-adaptation}; PLIP's frozen-domain gain is one-third of unfreezing's frozen-vs-adapted gain.
\end{itemize}
