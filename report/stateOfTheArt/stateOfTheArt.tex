\begin{itemize}
    \item Foundation models framing (Bommasani et al.~\cite{bommasani2021foundation}, 2021).
    \item CLIP and multimodal contrastive learning (Radford et al.~\cite{radford2021clip}, 2021; see also ALIGN~\cite{jia2021align}) --- the paradigm under study.
    \item Self-supervised visual pretraining --- contrastive (SimCLR~\cite{chen2020simclr}, MoCo~\cite{he2020moco}), self-distillation (DINO~\cite{caron2021dino}), and scaled SSL on diverse natural images (DINOv2~\cite{oquab2024dinov2}).
    \item Pathology shift: per-tile supervised CNNs $\to$ foundation models (2016--2024).
    \item Pathology-specific foundation models:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item TransPath~\cite{wang2022transpath} (2022) --- transformer + SSL.
        \item Phikon~\cite{filiot2023phikon} (2023) --- masked image modelling.
        \item PLIP~\cite{huang2023plip} (2023) --- CLIP on medical Twitter.
        \item CONCH~\cite{lu2024conch} (2024) --- larger pathology VLM.
        \item UNI~\cite{chen2024uni} (2024), BiomedCLIP~\cite{zhang2023biomedclip} (2023), MUSK~\cite{xiang2025musk} (2025) --- recent entrants.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
